
### 2. Disassemble original pantry

1. Remove the screws between the pantry door hinges and the metal frame.
2. Remove the shelves.
3. Remove the screws for the shelf supports on the left and right inside walls of the pantry.
4. Remove the fabric from the ceiling and back wall.
5. Remove the bottom cover (lowest shelf).
6. Remove the furring strips (backing that the fabric attached to).
7. The front metal frame (pantry opening) and the metal piece next to it need removed. For the metal frame there are two screws into the ceiling (these two screws will be reused later), two screws into the floor, five screws into the left wall, and five screws into the right wall. There are also five screws between the metal piece that goes from the floor to the ceiling to the right of the metal frame. There are plastic caps over the top of all these screws, then just pop off, you can use a finger nail or flat head screw driver to get under the edge of the cap. I found it difficult to get the screws between the metal frame and the metal piece to the right out, however if you loosen and remove all the screws that you can, you should be able to pivot the bottom of both the metal frame and metal piece to the right out at the same time. Once these two big metal pieces are out from the pantry walls it is easier to separate them and remove the screws.

***Note***: We will reinstall the metal piece that was to the right of the frame after a later step; after we use the provided jig to create the holes for the drawer slides.
