
### 2. Disassemble original pantry

1. Remove the pantry door by removing the screws between the pantry door hinges and the metal frame.
2. Remove the cover at the bottom of the front frame below the pantry door, this is held on by two cabinet latches, and will release if you pull it forward from the bottom and/or bottom.
3. Remove the metal frame (pantry opening) and the metal piece next to. For the metal frame there are two screws into the ceiling (these two screws will be reused later), two screws into the floor, five screws into the left wall, and five screws into the metal piece to the right of the frame. There are plastic caps over the top of all these screws, they just pop off; you can use a finger nail or flat head screw driver to get under the edge of the cap. I found it difficult to get the screws between the metal frame and the metal piece to the right out, however if you loosen and remove all the screws that you can, you should be able to pivot the bottom out, of both the metal frame and metal piece at the same time to remove them both. Once these two big metal pieces are out from the pantry walls it is easier to separate them and remove the screws.
4. Remove the shelves.
5. Remove the screws for the shelf supports on the left and right inside walls of the pantry.
6. Remove the fabric from the ceiling and back wall. There are small screws on the left and right of the back wall and at least one in front of the ceiling.
7. Remove the bottom cover (lowest shelf).
8. Remove the furring strips (backing that the fabric attached to).

***Note***: We will reinstall the metal piece that was to the right of the frame after a later step; after we use the provided jig to create the holes for the drawer slides.
