
### 3. Create the holes for the drawer slides

***Note***: With this kit we have provided a jig to create the holes in the pantry walls for the four drawer slides. The relationship between drawer slides is very important and in my testing I found that it was extremely difficult to create these holes and accurately. The drawer slides relative to each other need to be very close to the exact right distance (preferably perfect) and the drawer slides need to all be square to each other. Thankfully this jig and a process using shims (at a later step) makes that possible and is fairly easy!

1. This jig is pressed together using wood joinery. There are 14 pieces. Four of these pieces have holes in them that match the drawer slide holes. One piece (the smallest piece) has two holes that match the strike plate that the latch will catch on to keep the pantry drawer shut. The remaining pieces are used to connect all the other pieces, accurate space the drawer slides, and locate the jig within the pantry. The jig will need positioned before using the jig to transfer the holes to the pantry. The front left side of the jig will sit flush with the front left edge banding of the left pantry wall; the front of the top left drawer slide section, the front of the small latch piece, and the front of the bottom left drawer slide jig section will be flush with the edge branding on the front of the left pantry wall. The lower leg will touch the floor behind the heat exchanger. These surfaces locate/index the jig forward/back (Y axis), up/down (Z axis), and because the jig fits somewhat tightly into the pantry opening it is located/indexed left/right (X axis).
2. Assemble the jig using this picture as a reference, taking note that the vertical piece in the front left has another piece of wood joinery for the strike plate and the lower right drawer slide has another piece of wood joinery for the leg that locates off the floor. All of the wood joinery should push together tightly and bottom out. Be sure to handle the jig with care, if handled in a harsh manner it could break.
