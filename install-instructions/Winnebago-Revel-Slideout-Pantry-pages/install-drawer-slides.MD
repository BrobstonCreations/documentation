
### 4. Install drawer slides
1. Install the top left drawer slide using the screws included in the drawer slide packaging. Use five screws in the three larger holes along the center and the two near the back.
![PXL_20260822_155432430](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155432430.jpg)

2. Install the bottom left drawer slide.
![PXL_20260822_155310701](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155310701.jpg)

3. Depending on the order you decide to install, you may need to remove one fixed shelf from the pantry drawer which will be used to properly space the right drawer slide from the left.
![PXL_20260822_155507412](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155507412.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_155547887](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155547887.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_155607330](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155607330.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_155642605](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155642605.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_155711765](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155711765.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_155715253](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155715253.jpg)

4. Using ten M4 flat head machine screws and ten 1/8th thick spacers, install the drawer slide to the fixed shelf in this order: bolt -> drawer slide -> spacer -> shelf.
![PXL_20260822_155827343.MP](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155827343.MP.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_155945425](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_155945425.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_160108905](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_160108905.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_160315084~2](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_160315084~2.jpg)

5. Being sure to only install the screws just enough for the screws to hold in place, use five 1 1/2 inch phillips flat head screws to allow the drawer slide to hang from by the screws.
![PXL_20260822_160749953](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_160749953.jpg)

6. Begin sliding the shelf onto the left and right drawer slides. The goal is to add shims to the right drawer slide in the next step. Ideally we would tigthen these screws with the shelf slid all the way in, but that isn't possible. Tighten the right slide drawer screws enough for the drawer slide to close but not so tight that it begins pulling the slide too far to the right. If you slightly overtigten them, that is ok, we can loosen them while shimming.
![PXL_20260822_160843370](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_160843370.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_160917977](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_160917977.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_160934573](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_160934573.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_160953188](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_160953188.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_161011475](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_161011475.jpg)
![PXL_20260822_161041880](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_161041880.jpg)

***Note***: The goal of these next few steps is to space the right drawer slide away from the right pantry wall to create a distance from the right drawer slide to the left drawer slide that allows the shelf to slide in and out smoothly. Later in the install process I give steps to measure the shim thicknesses so that I can make and send you spacers based on these shims.

7. Locate the pack of shims. Shims are used in this orientation. As one shim is slid down the other shim is slid up, they create a consistent thickness, which allows us to tighten the drawer slide down against the shims at the correct distance to allow the drawer to slide in and out smoothly.
![PXL_20260822_161116491](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_161116491.jpg)

8. Starting at the back, just behind the screws, insert one shim from the top and insert one shim from the bottom. Press the top one down and the bottom one up until there is enough pressure that the shims are held in place.
![PXL_20260822_161203686](../Winnebago-Revel-Slideout-Pantry-images/PXL_20260822_161203686.jpg)

9. You may want to slide the shelf out far enough to further tighten the screws right next to the shim, but it is also possible to install all four sets of shims, and tigten of the screws after. Repeat this process for the other three sets of shims.
